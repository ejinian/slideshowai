SlideLabs

A tool that turns your job-site photos into finished TikTok slideshows and posts them for you. Ten minutes.

Ernest Jinian
→

Why this matters now

  • Marketing is the moat. Anyone can spin up an app or a website in a weekend. Attention is the part that can't be copied.
  • Social only gets bigger. Every year more of the buying decision happens on a feed, before anyone calls you.
  • Posting is the bottleneck. Pick photos, write copy, lay it out, upload. Twenty minutes for one post — so it doesn't happen.

What it does

One line in. A finished post out.

  • Type a sentence, or drop in photos from a job
  • It writes the hook and every caption, and lays out the slides
  • Sends straight to TikTok — post now or queue it for the week
  • Cuts the work by more than half. No design, no writing

What comes out

Real slides from the tool — hook first, then something a stranger can actually use. That's what gets watched, and it's where a certified arborist beats everyone else posting about trees.

  • “3 signs your oak is about to drop a limb”
  • “why topping a tree kills it”
  • “what a $4,000 removal actually pays for”
